What It Does

Every tool a stylist
would give you. Automated.

🤖
AI Style Profile

Tell the app your body shape, skin tone, city, climate, and style preferences. It builds a complete profile that powers every recommendation.

👗
Digital Closet

Snap a photo and the AI identifies garments, colors, fabric, category, and brand. Your entire wardrobe lives in your phone, organized and searchable.

🌤️
Weather-Aware Outfits

Every morning, get outfit suggestions built from your actual clothes, tuned to the weather, occasion, and your personal style. Work, date, casual — all covered.

💬
AI Stylist Chat

Ask anything: "Pack for a week in Goa", "Date night outfit with what I own", "What am I missing for winter?" The stylist knows your whole closet.

🪄
Inspiration Scanner

Screenshot a look from Instagram or Pinterest. The app breaks it into pieces, checks your closet, and shows you what to buy to recreate it.

🧍
Virtual Try-On

Preview outfits on your AI avatar, or upload your photo to see exactly how a new piece looks on you — before you buy it. No more guessing.
